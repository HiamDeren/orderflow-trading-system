00 · Hypothesis

Compare yesterday’s RTH value with overnight value to see what the market is accepting

The overnight session is the auction that continues right after yesterday’s RTH. Put the two profiles side by side on one chart; the intersection of the two value areas (overlap) is the area two independent sessions both consider fair. At 9:25 ET, the shape of the overlap tells you whether today is likely balance, migrating value, or imbalance. This playbook uses only TPO, VWAP, EMA, CVD and orderflow.

Both sessions use TPO VA (70% of time). This playbook trades balance–imbalance, and acceptance is measured by how long price stays in an area, so volume VA is not used. NQ’s official settlement is 16:00 ET; 16:14 also works if kept fixed. Monday: OVN starts Sunday 18:00 and is compared with Friday’s RTH.

Why TPO: this is a balance – imbalance playbook

  • Balance = the market spends time in one price area and both sides agree. On TPO that is the area where many periods stack (VA, TPOC).
  • Imbalance = price leaves that area and does not come back for long. On TPO that means single prints, tails, a thin profile.
  • TPO overlap = the area where both yesterday’s RTH and overnight spent a lot of time. Scenarios 1 and 6 are balance, scenarios 2 and 3 are balance in migration, scenarios 4 and 5 are imbalance waiting for RTH to confirm.
  • Acceptance / rejection is also measured in time: how many periods price stays beyond the level, not how many contracts.

Rules for every scenario

  • The zone is only location. Enter on an orderflow trigger: CVD divergence / absorption, delta flip, footprint. EMA 9/34 on M1 not against the trade.
  • VWAP RTH is the filter: flat = fading allowed; sloping with price riding a band = no fading, only go-with.
  • Acceptance = price stays beyond the level for ≥ 1 30-minute period (fast version) or 2 periods (standard), meaning TPO gets built there, not just a touch.
  • Max 2–3 trades. Stop at 11:30 ET (23:30 VN) or at the daily loss limit.
